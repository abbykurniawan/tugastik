01 • DISCOVER

A NATIONAL TREASURE

The Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system, located along the northeastern coast of Australia. It stretches for more than 2,300 kilometers and is made up of thousands of individual reefs and islands. The reef is home to a wide variety of marine life, including colorful fish, sea turtles, dolphins, and sharks. Its rich biodiversity makes it an important ecosystem and a valuable natural treasure.

The reef also plays an important role in the lives of people and communities around Australia. It attracts millions of visitors each year, supporting tourism and providing opportunities for research and education. However, maintaining the health of the Great Barrier Reef is important because damage to the reef can affect the many species and communities that depend on it.

04 • PROTECT

PROTECT THE REEF

We need to protect the Great Barrier Reef because it is home to many unique plants and animals and plays an important role in the ocean ecosystem. The reef provides food and shelter for thousands of marine species, including sea turtles, dugongs, and manta rays. It also supports local communities through tourism, fishing, and other activities. By protecting the reef, we can preserve its biodiversity and make sure that future generations can continue to enjoy and learn from this incredible natural environment.

05 • HISTORY

HISTORY OF THE REEF

The Great Barrier Reef has a long natural history that began millions of years ago. Its modern reef system developed as coral grew around islands and along Australia's northeastern coast. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have lived alongside and cared for the reef for thousands of years, using its resources for food and culture. European explorers first recorded the reef in the 18th century, with James Cook navigating through its waters in 1770. Over time, the Great Barrier Reef became an important area for scientific research, conservation, tourism, and education. Today, it is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Its incredible size and beauty make it one of the world's most remarkable natural wonders. Its colorful coral, clear waters, and diverse wildlife attract people from all over the world. The reef's unique beauty shows how amazing and valuable nature can be.

Visiting the Great Barrier Reef is an unforgettable experience because it allows you to see one of the world’s most amazing natural environments up close. You can explore colorful coral reefs, discover unique marine animals, and experience the beauty of the underwater world. A visit to the reef can also help you understand why protecting our oceans is so important. So, if you love nature, adventure, and discovering new places, the Great Barrier Reef is definitely a place worth visiting!
